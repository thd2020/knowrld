# Names and logos

The [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) covers this repository's code, and the packs' own licences cover their text. As section 6 of the Apache License says, neither grants rights to these names and marks, which identify the official game and the account behind it:

- **knowrld**, and the knowrld title mark;
- **Holarky**, and the Holarky mark (the leaning h with an outlined leg);
- **Phevere**.

## What you may do

- Write, check and publish your own packs in the knowrld pack format, and say they are "for knowrld" or "in the knowrld pack format".
- Say what your work is based on, for example "uses the knowrld pack validator".
- Use the names to refer to the official game, for example in articles, reviews and teaching.

## What a modified or derived product must do

- Use a different name and mark, so people do not take it for the official game.
- Not sign people in with the Holarky account, which serves the official apps only.
- Not suggest that Holarky made, checked or supports it.

## Asking

For anything else, such as using a name or mark in a product or a partnership, ask [thd2020](https://github.com/thd2020) first.
