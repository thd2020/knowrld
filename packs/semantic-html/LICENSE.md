Each excerpt in `sources/` records the page it comes from, its URL, the mdn/content commit it was cut from (dd70ed064388b0fac4338321f727c8840a508b64), and its line range; the excerpts are unmodified, including MDN's KumaScript macros. `fog.yaml` lists the titles and URLs of further MDN pages without quoting them. The structure of the pack follows MDN Learn web development.
